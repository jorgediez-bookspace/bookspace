Privacy Policy

BookSpace

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Application Package: com.jorgediez.bookspace • Developer: Jorge Diez

1. Introduction

Welcome to BookSpace ("we", "our", or "us"). BookSpace is a minimalist, distraction-free reading companion designed for Android, iOS, and web previews.

We believe your reading habits, thoughts, notes, and personal reflections are deeply personal. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, where it is stored, and your rights in accordance with the Google Play Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Local-First Privacy Philosophy

100% On-Device Data Storage

BookSpace is built on a Local-First / Offline-First architecture. Your library, reading progress, focus timer logs, and notes are stored strictly on your physical device in sandboxed application storage.

We do not operate remote servers that track your reading behavior, sell your habits to advertisers, or monitor the books you read. The app functions completely offline.

3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Account & Profile Information

When you set up an account, you may provide an email address, display name, and avatar image. This information is saved locally on your device to personalize your reading interface and milestone cards.

B. User-Generated Reading Content

Book titles, authors, total page counts, current reading page, shelf classifications (Currently Reading, Want to Read, Finished), focus session durations, and user-written notes are stored on your device to calculate your progress and power the notes journal.

C. Device Permissions

  • Photo Library Access: Requested only when you explicitly choose to select a custom avatar photo from your device's photo gallery via the system image picker. BookSpace accesses only the single image you choose.
  • Foreground Audio Playback: Used solely to allow ambient reading soundscapes (rain, fireplace, library) to continue playing uninterrupted when the screen locks.
  • Internet Access: Used to query public book catalog metadata via the Open Library and Google Books APIs, and to stream royalty-free ambient audio tracks.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No precise or approximate GPS location data.
  • No device contacts, address books, or call/SMS logs.
  • No microphone audio recordings (the app plays audio, never records it).
  • No advertising identifiers (GAID, IDFA) or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No financial, credit card, or payment data.

5. Third-Party Services

BookSpace interacts only with a minimal set of trusted services:

  • Open Library & Google Books API: Anonymous book search queries (title/author) to retrieve covers and page counts. No user identifiers are transmitted.
  • Native Sharing: Progress milestone cards generated locally on-device and handed directly to your chosen social apps (WhatsApp, Instagram, X) via the native OS share sheet.

6. Data Deletion & Retention (Your Rights)

One-Tap In-App Account Wipe

You have complete control over your data at all times. You can permanently erase all stored reading data and your account directly from within the app:

Settings → Danger Zone → Tap "Delete Account"

Uninstalling the application from your device will also delete all local databases and cached content.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

BookSpace is a general utility reading app and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
