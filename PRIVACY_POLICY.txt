1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to BookSpace ("we", "our", or "us"). BookSpace is a minimalist,
distraction-free reading companion application designed for mobile devices 
(Android and iOS) and web previews.

We believe that your reading habits, thoughts, notes, and personal reflections
are deeply personal. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting
your privacy, detailing what information is collected, how it is used, where
it is stored, and your rights regarding your data in accordance with the Google
Play Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the
California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BookSpace is built on a "Local-First / Offline-First" architecture:
- Your reading library, progress, focus timer history, and private reading 
  notes are stored locally on your device in secure, sandboxed application storage.
- We do not operate remote servers that track your reading behavior, sell your 
  habits to advertisers, or monitor the books you read.
- You can use the application completely offline without an active internet
  connection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND HOW WE USE IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Account & Profile Information
- Information Provided: When you create an account, you may provide an email
  address, a display name, and select or upload a profile avatar.
- Purpose: To personalize your reading experience, identify your milestone cards,
  and organize your reading logs.
- Storage: Stored locally on your device within the application's protected
  database.

B. User-Generated Reading Content
- Information Provided: Book titles, author names, total page counts, current
  reading progress, custom shelf classifications (Currently Reading, Want to
  Read, Finished), reading session durations, and user-written notes/quotes.
- Purpose: To calculate your completion percentages, display reading statistics,
  and power the distraction-free focus timer and notes journal.
- Storage: Kept strictly on your local device.

C. Device Permissions & Media Access
- Photo Library / Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Photo Picker):
  Requested ONLY when you explicitly choose to select a custom avatar photo from
  your device's gallery. BookSpace accesses only the single image you choose.
  We do not scan, collect, or upload your photo library.
- Background Audio Playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / WAKE_LOCK):
  Used solely to allow the built-in ambient reading soundscapes (such as rain,
  fireplace, or forest wind) to continue playing uninterrupted while you focus
  or when your device screen is locked.
- Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE):
  Used to:
  1) Perform live book catalog queries via the public Open Library and Google
     Books APIs when you search for a book to add.
  2) Stream royalty-free ambient audio tracks for the focus reading mode.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We respect your digital boundaries. BookSpace does NOT collect:
- Precise or coarse GPS location data.
- Device contacts, address books, or call/SMS logs.
- Audio recordings (we play audio; we never record from the microphone).
- Biometric information.
- Advertising identifiers (GAID / IDFA) for third-party advertising or profiling.
- Financial, credit card, or payment information.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & EXTERNAL LIBRARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To provide core functionality without compromising privacy, BookSpace utilizes
a minimal number of trusted third-party services:

1. Open Library API (Internet Archive) & Google Books API
   When you search for a book in the app, your search query string (e.g., author
   or title) is transmitted to these public catalog endpoints to retrieve cover
   art, publication metadata, and page counts. No personally identifiable 
   information (PII) or user account information is attached to these queries.

2. Expo & EAS (Expo Application Services)
   The app is built using the Expo framework. Expo may collect basic, anonymous
   technical crash diagnostics or device platform types to ensure stability and
   deliver app updates.

3. Native Sharing (Android / iOS Share Sheet)
   When you tap "Share Milestone" or "Share Note", the app uses native device
   sharing APIs (via react-native-view-shot and expo-sharing) to generate a
   progress card image and hand it off directly to whichever app you choose
   (such as WhatsApp, Instagram, or X). BookSpace does not transmit this image
   to any external cloud server.

We DO NOT include third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, behavioral trackers,
or data broker analytics within the app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION (YOUR RIGHTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because your data is stored locally on your device:

A. Automatic Deletion via Uninstall
- If you uninstall the BookSpace application from your device, all local databases,
  cached files, library books, and reading notes will be automatically deleted
  by the operating system.

B. In-App One-Tap Account & Data Wipe
- You can permanently wipe your account and all associated reading data at any
  time directly from inside the app:
  1. Open BookSpace.
  2. Navigate to the "Settings" tab.
  3. Scroll down to the "Danger Zone".
  4. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm.
- This immediately and irrecoverably erases your user profile, book catalog,
  reading notes, session statistics, and settings from your device storage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. SECURITY OF YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We take the security of your data seriously:
- Data is isolated within the sandboxed directory provided by Android/iOS, which
  prevents other installed applications from inspecting or accessing your data.
- All network communications (such as book catalog lookups and audio streaming)
  use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA / GDPR-K COMPLIANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BookSpace is a general audience utility application and is not directed to
children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly
collect, solicit, or store personal information from children. If you believe
that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at
the email address below, and we will promptly ensure the data is deleted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. INTERNATIONAL USER RIGHTS (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Regardless of your geographical location, you have the following rights:
- Right to Access: You can view all stored books, notes, and profile details
  directly on the app screens at any time.
- Right to Rectification: You can edit your display name, avatar, notes, and 
  page counts directly within the app.
- Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten": You can delete individual books,
  notes, or execute a full account wipe using the in-app "Delete Account" feature.
- Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user for 
  exercising their privacy rights.
- "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": We do not sell, rent, or share
  your personal data with any third parties for monetary or commercial gain.
